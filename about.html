Indigo Blue is the indigo dye and textile design practice by Marin native Keely Ganong.

Every piece is one of one, inspired by Marin County's open spaces, with a focus on blue hues, collage-like pattern making, and upcycled fabrics.

Limited drops, made in small batches. Join the list to be notified when the next collection is ready.
